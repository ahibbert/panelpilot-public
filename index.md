# Panel Pilot public information

Panel Pilot is a private, local-first iPhone and iPad reader for user-owned manga, comics, and webtoons. Automatic guided reading detects panels on-device; page images do not leave the device for detection.

Panel Pilot includes no content catalogue, default source, account, proxy, advertising, tracking, or silent telemetry. Users may import owned CBZ/ZIP archives. Advanced compatibility settings permit manual entry of a third-party extension repository URL; installation and technical identity checks do not establish content rights or service permission.

## App Review fixtures

The [controlled review fixture v4](review-fixture-v4/README.html) contains original, non-adult artwork and a DEX-only extension used to demonstrate Panel Pilot's manual repository, security review, rights disclosure, resource-backed settings, browsing, explicitly permitted download, content controls, and guided-reading flows. Its coverless metadata cases prove that mature opt-in cannot unlock graphic-sexual or prohibited graphic-violence titles. It is not an extension catalogue.

The same original CC0 page is available as a [deterministic CBZ import fixture](review-fixture/files/) for the document-picker, `ComicInfo.xml`, offline-reading, and local panel-detection review path. It contains no script, extension, credential, remote URL, or executable content.
