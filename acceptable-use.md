# Acceptable use — pre-release draft

Status: published for internal testing and review preparation. This policy is not yet effective. Specialist review and an effective date are required before release.

Panel Pilot is operated by Aydin Daniel Sareff-Hibbert in New South Wales, Australia. The final policy is intended to be governed by the laws of New South Wales, subject to mandatory applicable law and specialist review.

Panel Pilot is for reading material that the user owns, is licensed to access, is in the public domain, or is otherwise lawfully available to that user.

A user must not use Panel Pilot to:

- infringe copyright, privacy, trademark, or other rights;
- bypass a paywall, subscription, access control, geographic restriction, or technical protection measure;
- access a service contrary to applicable law or binding service terms;
- distribute copied material without authority; or
- install or operate malicious, deceptive, or abusive extension code.

Third-party extensions are user-supplied compatibility software. Their presence, signature, installation, or technical operation does not mean that Panel Pilot has reviewed or authorised their content, operator, or source service. Users must review the repository, domains, service terms, and their own authority before enabling an extension.

Panel Pilot may disable networking, aggregated discovery, or downloading for a repository, package, signer, domain, or source when reasonably required for security, rights, platform-compliance, or legal reasons. These controls should preserve local imports, existing downloads, and reading progress where legally and technically possible. Questions or reports may be sent privately to [panelpilotcontact@proton.me](mailto:panelpilotcontact@proton.me). The final appeal process remains subject to specialist review.
