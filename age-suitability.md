# Age suitability and content controls

Panel Pilot is intended for adults and is planned for an App Store 18+ age-rating override. The application supplies a reader, not a catalogue, and users manually choose local archives and third-party extension repositories.

Mature-content discovery is off by default. Mainstream or mixed catalogues and mature non-graphic titles require explicit opt-in. Repositories, packages, domains, or remote titles classified as pornographic, hentai, or graphically sexually explicit are blocked before covers or pages load. Adult-only repositories are unsupported even after mature-content opt-in.

Panel Pilot does not inspect or classify private archives imported through the iOS document picker. Users remain responsible for content they own or are authorised to access. An 18+ rating does not permit content that the App Store prohibits.

The release safety policy is signed, versioned, replay-protected, and capable of revoking repositories, packages, signing certificates, domains, and unsafe versions. New installations and updates require a fresh policy. Extension networking stops after the configured expiry grace period, while already downloaded and local content stays readable.

This page remains subject to specialist legal and App Review advice.
