# Age suitability and content controls

Panel Pilot is intended for adults. Its App Store Connect declaration currently calculates to 18+ globally (A18 in Brazil and 19+ in the Republic of Korea). The application supplies a reader, not a catalogue, and users manually choose local archives and third-party extension repositories.

Mature-content discovery is off by default. Mainstream or mixed catalogues and mature non-graphic titles require explicit opt-in. Repositories, packages, domains, or remote titles classified as pornographic, hentai, graphically sexually explicit, gore/guro, prolonged graphic violence, or sadistic violence are blocked before covers, chapters, or pages load. Adult-only repositories are unsupported even after mature-content opt-in.

Panel Pilot does not inspect or classify private archives imported through the iOS document picker. Users remain responsible for content they own or are authorised to access. Apple classifies graphically sexual content and prolonged graphic or sadistic realistic violence as unrated and ineligible for App Store publication; the 18+ rating is not an exemption.

The release safety policy is signed, versioned, replay-protected, and capable of revoking repositories, packages, signing certificates, domains, and unsafe versions. New installations and updates require a fresh policy. Extension networking stops after the configured expiry grace period, while already downloaded and local content stays readable.

This page remains subject to specialist legal and App Review advice.
