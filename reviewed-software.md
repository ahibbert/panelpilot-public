# Reviewed-software index

This index is deliberately separate from third-party repositories. It lists only software for which Panel Pilot has recorded identity, provenance, licence or authorisation evidence, network scope, and a repeatable technical review. It is not a general extension catalogue or a recommendation service.

This is currently the only rights-reviewed extension identity. No independent third-party extension, repository, domain, or content service is presently represented as verified, authorised, official, safe, or lawful by Panel Pilot.

## What “reviewed” means

A listing requires evidence tied to the exact package and signing-certificate identity. At minimum, review considers the software's signer, reproducible or otherwise documented provenance, declared network hosts, licence or written authorisation, content-rights basis, security behaviour, and a contact capable of responding to notices. Status can be narrowed, suspended, or revoked through the signed safety policy.

Cryptographic signature verification alone proves only that APK releases share a signing identity. It does not prove copyright ownership, permission from a content service, or authority to distribute works.

## Requesting review or reporting a problem

There is no paid placement and no automatic approval. Operators or rights holders may contact [panelpilotcontact@proton.me](mailto:panelpilotcontact@proton.me). Reports and appeals follow the [notice review and appeal process](appeals.html).
